# Robust Regression & Hypothesis Testing Examples

**Question:** How do robust standard errors and formal hypothesis tests change inference across different regression applications?

**Data:** Multiple datasets used for illustration, including voting/expenditure data, birthweight data, and household net financial assets.

**Methods:**  
- OLS with robust standard errors (`vce(robust)`)  
- Hypothesis testing (`test`)  
- Log transformations and reparameterization  

**Examples included:**  
- Voting outcomes and campaign expenditures  
- Birthweight determinants (cigarettes, parity, family income)  
- Net financial assets and income/age  

**Output:** Stata log demonstrating robust regression and hypothesis testing on several datasets.
