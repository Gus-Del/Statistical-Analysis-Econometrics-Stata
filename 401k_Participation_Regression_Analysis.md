# 401(k) Plan Participation

**Question:** What factors are associated with higher employee participation rates in 401(k) plans?

**Data:** Firm-level observations on 401(k) plans, including participation rate, employer match rate, firm size, plan age, and whether the 401(k) is the firm’s only plan.

**Methods:** Summary statistics and OLS regression.

**Key variables:**  
- `prate` — participation rate  
- `mrate` — employer match rate  
- `totpart` / `totelg` — participants and eligible employees  
- `age` — age of the plan  
- `totemp` / `ltotemp` — firm size (and log)  
- `sole` — whether the 401(k) is the sole plan  

**Output:** Stata log showing data exploration and regression results.
